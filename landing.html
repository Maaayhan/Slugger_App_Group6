The Problem

Group chats don't cut it for fitness goals

Ian and his friends used a WhatsApp group to track weekly wellness goals. It worked — but it was chaotic. Slugger was built to fix exactly this.

💬

Messy & Unstructured

Scrolling through hundreds of messages to track who did what is exhausting and error-prone.

📊

No Analytics

No way to see trends, visualize contributions, or understand team performance over time.

😴

Lacks Engagement

Without gamification, motivation fades quickly. Punishments exist but aren't enforced fairly.

What Slugger Does

Every feature built for accountability

🎯

Custom Goal Setting

Set personalized weekly targets across physical health, mental wellness, and long-term aspirations. Fully flexible, fully yours.

👥

Team Collaboration

Create or join groups, work toward shared targets, and hold each other accountable with real-time progress visibility.

🏆

Rewards & Forfeits

Earn points and unlock achievements for every logged activity. Miss your goal? Face the consequences — 500 push-ups, anyone?

📈

Data-Driven Insights

Visual dashboards and analytics help you understand your patterns, track streaks, and stay motivated week after week.

⚡

One-Tap Logging

Log any activity with a single button press. Running, yoga, meditation, journaling — instant contribution to your team goal.

🔒

Secure Auth System

JWT-based authentication with email verification, bcrypt password hashing, and secure session management built-in.

How It Works

Up and running in minutes

1

Sign Up

Create your account with email verification for a secure, verified identity.

2

Form a Team

Create a group or join one with a code. Challenge your friends to compete.

3

Set Weekly Goals

Define your group's target point count for the week across all activity types.

4

Track & Win

Log activities, watch the leaderboard, and celebrate hitting your goal together.
